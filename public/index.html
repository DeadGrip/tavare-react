The Designer

Unrated. Not unknown.

Elara Voss has been restoring and reworking vintage silver for eleven years from a single-room studio, mostly for private clients who found her by word of mouth. She has never shown in a gallery, never taken a rating, and turned down two retail partnerships because they wanted her to stop hand-numbering the pieces.

"I don't need a rating to know a piece is right. I need forty years of silver telling me so." — E. Voss

Every piece that leaves her studio is inspected, restored using period-appropriate methods, and assigned a permanent number before it's offered here.

Provenance
