Where The Design Comes From

Four ideas from Indian craft, inside one set

Every detail of the design is drawn from a real technique or tradition — nothing here is decorative for its own sake.

Enamel work · Rajasthan

Meenakari

The old technique of firing colour into metal, usually hidden on the reverse of a piece. We use it as a hidden signature inside the chain's connector fitting.

Stone setting · North India

Kundan

Stones set flush rather than raised on prongs, so the setting reads as one smooth surface — the basis for how our connector is built.

Stone screens · Mughal architecture

Jali Lattice

The pierced stone lattices found in Mughal-era windows and screens, translated into the fine openwork along the upper edge of each earring.

Drop earring · Pan-India

Jhumka Silhouette

The bell-shaped drop earring worn across India for generations. Ours softens that silhouette into a slimmer, more minimal line.
