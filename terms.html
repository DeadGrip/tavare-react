Terms, Shipping & Returns

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Reserving a set

Reserving a set through this site is free and does not commit you to a purchase. It secures your place in the Series I queue, in the order reservations are received. No payment is taken at reservation — we'll contact you by email with pricing and payment details once Series I production opens.

Series I availability

Series I is limited to 50 numbered sets. Once fully reserved, that exact design and finish combination will not be repeated within the same series. We'll let reserved customers know if a design sells out before their turn.

Shipping

Sets are made to order after Series I closes, by our jewellers in India. Once made, we ship via tracked courier to any address in the EU, with delivery estimated at 10–14 days from dispatch. Shipping costs and any applicable import duties for your country will be shown before you're asked to pay.

Returns & exchanges

Because each set is made to order and individually numbered, we aim to offer a return window of 14 days from delivery for unworn items in original packaging, in line with EU consumer distance-selling rules. Contact studio@tavarestudio.com to arrange a return once your order has shipped — full terms will be confirmed at the point of sale.

Pricing

All prices will be shown in EUR. Catalogue prices shown before Series I opens are indicative and may be confirmed or adjusted before production begins; you'll always see final pricing before being asked to pay.

These terms are a plain-language summary for a pre-launch business and should be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before Series I opens for real orders and payments.
