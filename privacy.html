Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Tavaré ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through tavarestudio.com, why, and what rights you have over it.

What we collect

When you reserve a set on the Reserve page, we collect the name, email address, and finish preference you provide. We use this only to:

  • Assign and confirm your place in the Series I waitlist
  • Email you about the status of your reservation
  • Reply to any message you send us directly by email

We do not collect payment details anywhere on this site — reserving a set is free and is not an order.

Where it's stored

Reservation data is stored in our database (hosted by Supabase, an EU-capable data platform) and reservation confirmation emails are sent via Resend. Neither is used for advertising, and we don't sell or share your details with third parties.

Cookies

This site uses one small, essential cookie-equivalent (stored in your browser's local storage) to remember that you've seen the cookie notice, so it doesn't reappear on every visit. We don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.

Your rights

If you're in the EU/EEA or UK, you have the right under GDPR to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, email studio@tavarestudio.com — we reply within two working days.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

This policy is a plain-language summary intended for a small pre-launch business and should be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before relying on it for a live store handling real customer data or payments.
