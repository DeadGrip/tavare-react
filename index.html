The Set

Three parts. Four outcomes.

×2

Drop earrings

Each earring closes into a small interlocking hook, so the pair can be worn on their own or joined together.

×1

Convertible chain

One length of chain that sits as a necklace, wraps as a bracelet, or holds the joined earrings as a pendant.

×1

Connector fitting

A single hidden clasp point where the earrings attach to the chain — the mechanism behind all four looks.
